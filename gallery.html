A chip-level repair involves micro-soldering on the motherboard to replace failed ICs, MOSFETs, capacitors or other components. The board is cleaned, tested and reassembled before being returned. This work is done in a controlled workshop environment with ESD-safe tools.
Yes. Smart Technology services laptops, desktops and motherboards from major brands including HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer, Asus, Toshiba, Sony, Samsung and Apple. We diagnose the fault first, then confirm compatibility before fitting any replacement parts.
